## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-17-01 | Tampering | `read_apkg_cards` | mitigate | Compute SHA-256 before/after read and assert no mutation; use `TemporaryDirectory` only. |
| T-17-02 | Information Disclosure | `AuditIssue.evidence` | mitigate | Store bounded excerpts and basename-only source path metadata; never include absolute paths. |
| T-17-03 | Denial of Service | APKG zip/SQLite reader | mitigate | Read only `collection.anki2`, reject missing/invalid structures, and do not extract media payloads. |
