## Common Pitfalls
- Treating a configured API key as approval to spend. This plan may record alias presence only and cannot validate or persist secret values.
- Filling unknown model, budget, latency, or Azure region fields with reasonable defaults. Missing exact operator decisions stop execution.
- Enabling word or sentence audio route records before live catalog/profile/sample authority exists. Audio policy stays disabled here.
- Letting provider-review authority approve generated Korean or Portuguese content. It approves only the route/policy snapshot; AI linguistic review still applies to exact content later.
- Reusing old Plan 32-25 sidecar syntax. The current CLI uses `--authority-file`, not `--authority` or `--sidecar`.

- Authority validation is required before the artifacts can be consumed by a later pilot execution or DB/job-binding plan.
- Any later change from disabled audio routes to enabled audio routes requires separate audio/profile authority and a new hash-bound policy decision.
