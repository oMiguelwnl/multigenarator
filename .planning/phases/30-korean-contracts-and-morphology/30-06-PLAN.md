## Requirements Covered

- **KNLP-01:** persisted fingerprint and NFC/script evidence are required after reload.
- **KNLP-02:** inflected morpheme matching and every inconclusive fail-closed state are enforced.
- **KMODE-02:** generic validation/repair behavior remains unchanged.

## Must-Haves

1. Korean matching precedes every generic route.
2. Only `matched` passes.
3. Identity survives repository restoration.
4. Retry/regeneration cannot bypass the gate.

## Anti-Goals

- No runtime, CLI, provider, audio, export, or quality-policy work.

## Hard Boundaries

- Do not reconstruct identity from display form, lemma, or definitions.
- Do not call Tatoeba for Korean.
- Do not change generic validators to match Korean policy.

## Evidence Contract

- Validator tests use injected counting fakes to prove zero generic calls for `ko`.
- Positive/negative cases cover inflection, homographs, compounds, ambiguity, OOV, unavailable, missing, malformed, and drift.
- Generation and regeneration tests verify identity propagation and persisted review status across retries.
- Existing non-Korean test cases remain green in the same files.

## Common Pitfalls

- Placing Korean after Japanese/Mandarin/generic early returns.
- Using `heuristic_match` when Kiwi is unavailable.
- Restoring all candidate fields except `korean_identity`.
- Skipping identity on retry or regeneration.

## Stop-And-Challenge

- Stop on any Korean fallback reachability or lost identity after reload.

## Approval Gates

- Consensus, fingerprint, generic validators, and provider fallbacks are locked.
