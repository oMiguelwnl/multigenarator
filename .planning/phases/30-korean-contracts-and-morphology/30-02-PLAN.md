## Requirements Covered

- **KNLP-01:** NFC, explicit pinned analyzer configuration, typed lemma/POS/morpheme evidence, and required real goldens.
- **KNLP-02:** ordered eojeol-bounded signatures, homograph separation, inflection matching, and fail-closed result states.
- **KMODE-02:** unsupported voice/Tatoeba behavior, lazy Korean-only construction, and no mutation of existing providers or generic morphology.

## Must-Haves

1. Unicode and identity invariants are enforced by construction.
2. Analyzer configuration and versions are fully fingerprinted.
3. Real Kiwi goldens cover every approved linguistic category.
4. Consensus matching cannot fall through to heuristics.
5. Failures are typed, deterministic, and privacy-safe.
6. Korean voice/Tatoeba requests fail safely without inventing provider capability.

## Anti-Goals

- No lexical-source approval or production sense assignment.
- No voice registration, synthesis, Tatoeba provider code, or network request.
- No persistence or source-mode orchestration.
- No generic morphology refactor.
- No content generation, translation, audio, export, or UI.

## Hard Boundaries

- Use NFC, never NFKC, for Korean canonical content.
- Normalize POS suffixes such as `-I`/`-R` to their base POS for comparison while retaining raw tags only as safe diagnostics.
- Exclude `J*`, `E*`, punctuation, web/unknown, and appended-coda artifacts from lexical signatures; retain `XSV`/`XSA`.
- Keep ordered signatures and one-eojeol boundaries; do not compare unordered sets.

## Evidence Contract

- Domain tests prove canonical equivalence, forbidden-range rejection, key separation by POS/sense, and impossible invalid resolved identities.
- Voice/Tatoeba tests prove controlled failure/no-result and zero Korean provider calls while preserving existing mappings.
- Real adapter tests exercise the installed packages for noun+particle, regular predicate, contextual irregular predicate, adjectival predicate, compound predicate, homograph, and NFC/NFD cases.
- The stable real-Kiwi fixture `알리오올리오가 진짜 맛있는 집` is analyzed with the exact pinned profile; both top alternatives must contain the actual vendor token `form == "알리오올리오"` with `Token.oov is True`, and the project analysis plus target match must remain non-passing.
- Factories/test doubles are used only for import failure, model-construction failure, or runtime-unavailable branches; they never fabricate OOV tokens, linguistic positives, or ambiguity evidence.
- A test proves one matching and one non-matching analysis yields `ambiguous`, never `matched`.

## Common Pitfalls

- Turning `results[0]` into identity evidence.
- Adding a guessed voice or `kor` mapping to satisfy enum exhaustiveness.
- Comparing a flattened signature and losing `word_position` boundaries.
- Dropping `XSV`/`XSA`, which collapses `공부하다` into the noun `공부`.
- Including endings or particles in a target signature.
- Echoing text, vendor token reprs, or model paths in unavailable details.

## Stop-And-Challenge

- Stop on pinned-API mismatch or unstable real golden; do not compensate with hand-written Korean rules.
- Stop if a design cannot represent two analysis alternatives and explicit disagreement.
- Stop if a resolved identity can be instantiated with unknown POS/sense or absent fingerprint.

## Approval Gates

- Analyzer pins, options, and top-two consensus are locked user decisions.
- No approval exists for additional dialects, correction behavior, user dictionaries, or a production lexical source.
