## Requirements Covered

- **KHAN-01:** complete modern inventory and required pedagogical/media slot data.
- **KHAN-02:** H0 bootstrap and H0-H10 strict evidence in actual source records.
- **KPRO-02:** fixed P0-P13 coverage declarations and inherited orthographic identity contract consumed by Plan 31-03.

## Must-Haves

1. Every locked Hangul stage/category is represented and scanner-verifiable.
2. Every actual Hangul strict candidate passes the shared one-unknown engine.
3. The pronunciation skeleton is structurally ready for complete P0-P13 population without duplicating Hangul identity.

## Anti-Goals

- No media acquisition, synthesized raw glyphs, approval records, templates, export artifacts, or P0-P13 learner-record population.

## Hard Boundaries

- Cite authoritative source identity; do not make remote requests at load/export time.
- Keep source records richer than note fields.
- Keep canonical, normative, surface, optional IPA, register/context, and source citations distinct.

## Evidence Contract

- Tests load the committed files, assert exact coverage/count invariants, and mutate each class of evidence.
- The production source packs validate structurally while every unresolved human claim remains non-exportable.
- No checked-in row can transition to approved through a test helper.

## Common Pitfalls

- Treating H/P stage labels as atomic concepts.
- Duplicating coda concepts between Hangul and pronunciation.
- Filling missing P11-P13 evidence with unsupported forms or machine-generated IPA.
- Using Compatibility Jamo in canonical/hash fields.

## Stop-And-Challenge

- Stop on any pressure to invent source claims, drop a required family, or label an incomplete candidate as a strict learner card.

## Approval Gates

- Orthography, phonetics, Portuguese, licensing, and playback approvals remain pending: Plan 31-04 defines the gates, 31-07 defines exact requests, 31-08 through 31-10 implement/test the automatic boundary, and 31-11/12 require genuine evidence plus separate activation authorization.
