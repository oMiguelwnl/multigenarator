## Requirements Covered

- **KPRO-01:** exact nine-field candidate values and media-slot identities for every pronunciation item.
- **KPRO-02:** complete P0-P13 dependency/category coverage, one unknown, and active-rule accounting.

## Must-Haves

1. Every locked P-stage/category is populated and scanner-verifiable.
2. Every strict pronunciation candidate passes the shared one-unknown and active-rule engine.
3. Missing specialist, Portuguese, auditory, or media truth remains an explicit blocker, never synthetic approval.

## Anti-Goals

- No graph/Hangul rewrites, review approvals, media acquisition, templates, exporters, providers, or runtime integration.

## Hard Boundaries

- Reuse registry/H7/H8 orthographic IDs exactly; never create pronunciation-local duplicates.
- Keep normative, surface, optional IPA, register/context, and source citations distinct.
- Source records stay richer than the exact nine learner fields.

## Evidence Contract

- Focused tests prove P0-P7 before P8-P13 is populated.
- The final task runs the unfiltered curriculum suite across all three manifests.
- Mutation tests reject omitted categories, fused targets, forward edges, forged unknowns, missing active rules, hash drift, unsupported scripts, and premature approval.

## Common Pitfalls

- Treating a P-stage label or “all batchim” as one concept.
- Recomputing orthographic identity instead of referencing the shared registry.
- Filling unavailable P11-P13 or IPA evidence with an unsupported generated form.
- Letting nine-field completeness imply qualified review.

## Stop-And-Challenge

- Stop on any need to invent linguistic truth, weaken strict evidence, alter completed Hangul identity, or use automation as phonetics approval.

## Approval Gates

- This plan creates reviewable candidates only; Plan 31-04 defines gates, Plans 31-07 through 31-10 complete pending requests and automatic machinery, and Plans 31-11/12 bind genuine canonical evidence and authorization.
