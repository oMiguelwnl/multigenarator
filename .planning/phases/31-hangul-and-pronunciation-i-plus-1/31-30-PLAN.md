# Phase 31: Hangul and Pronunciation i+1 - Plan 30

## Objective

Implement the global AI linguistic review contract and apply it to every exact v2 Hangul and pronunciation record without impersonating human authority.

## Context

- `.planning/AI-LINGUISTIC-REVIEW-POLICY.md`
- `31-APPROACH.md`
- `31-25-SUMMARY.md`
- `data/korean_foundations/current-candidate.json`

## Requirements Covered

- KHAN-01, KHAN-02, KPRO-01, KPRO-02

## Must-Haves

1. Closed, versioned AI actor/review/validator/consensus schemas.
2. Two-pass standard and three-pass critical review with no majority override.
3. Exact 139-record aggregate with explicit blockers rather than invented approval.

## Anti-Goals

- No human qualification emulation, rights decision, media generation, activation, export, provider call, or publication.

## Hard Boundaries

- Review agents receive fixed projections only and have no filesystem/network/mutation tools.
- Existing human evidence fields remain reserved and cannot accept AI actor data.

## Evidence Contract

- Contract and consensus tests pass; aggregate binds policy/candidate/request/validator hashes and exact coverage counts.
- Recovery addendum: AI review surfaced candidate-data blockers in learner-facing Hangul labels and Korean pronunciation projections. Those fixes are included in the AI lane scope only because the aggregate cannot honestly pass against the previous candidate bytes.

## Common Pitfalls

- Reusing `approved`, accepting majority vote, trusting self-confidence, hiding same-model review correlation, or carrying review across changed bytes.

## Stop-And-Challenge

- Keep the record blocked if review passes disagree or source evidence cannot support a claim.

## Approval Gates

- None; all work is local, bounded, and non-mutating outside the AI evidence directory and code/tests.
