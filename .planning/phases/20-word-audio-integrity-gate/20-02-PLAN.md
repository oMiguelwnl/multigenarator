|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-20-04 | Tampering | `GenerateAudioItemsService._materialize_asset` | mitigate | Validate reusable WORD assets with `audio_integrity` before reuse; synthesize prepared assets on mismatch. |
| T-20-05 | Repudiation | `GenerateAudioItemsResult.reused_items` | mitigate | Count mismatched reusable assets as regenerated, not reused, so execution evidence reflects what happened. |
| T-20-06 | Denial of Service | audio regeneration | accept | Regeneration may fail if the adapter fails; later export gates block failed or mismatched assets with clear diagnostics. |
