# Phase 33 Plan 03: Local Highlight Morphology and Private Artifact Separation

<objective>
Strengthen Korean highlight extraction and define separate private excerpt, safe candidate, provider-context, and microexample artifact contracts.

Purpose: KPERS-02 requires authentic reading context to remain private while preserving morphology and producing a distinct reviewable learner example.
Output: Frozen highlight privacy contracts, Korean-first extraction behavior, and leakage/fallback regression tests.
</objective>

## Anti-Goals
- No provider disclosure, excerpt-as-example projection, private provenance output, generic Korean fallback, or strict label for authentic text.

## Hard Boundaries
- Analyze the full bounded excerpt locally with source-backed Korean morphology before filtering and keep excerpt/context/microexample types separate.

## Evidence Contract
- Mutation/golden/sentinel tests must prove attached forms and one-syllable words survive while all safe/error/export-shaped projections omit private values.

## Common Pitfalls
- Running token-length filters first, deduplicating by lemma alone, or serializing the private model through an ordinary candidate path.

## Stop-And-Challenge
- Stop on exact-text leakage, analyzer failure followed by generic extraction, or any request to merge authentic context with learner output.

## Approval Gates
- No remote-processing authority is granted here; Plan 04 owns the exact capability boundary.

## Leverage Review
- Lost: ordinary candidate-field convenience. Kept: local import/resolver. Gained: typed private and safe artifact separation.
