# Phase 33 Plan 16: Non-Vacuous Personal Processing and Recoverable Audio Publication

<objective>
Run exact custom/highlight processing and policy review, then publish all required audio through a crash-recoverable reservation/staging/journal protocol.

Purpose: Produce real ready personal siblings and immutable audio without a filesystem/DB consistency gap.
Output: Four local roots and four atomic evidence output+sidecar pairs.
</objective>

## Anti-Goals
- No source/migration changes, empty-family waiver, alternate profile/path, shared byte file, history deletion, prepared rows, activation, publication, APKG or delivery.

## Hard Boundaries
- Calls require current authority; DB reservation commits before TTS; no call occurs in a transaction; only ephemeral staging may be removed after successful reconciliation.

## Evidence Contract
- Exact process invocation events, positive eligible/ready counts, AI policy evidence, unique paths, reservation/journal transitions, byte hashes and crash recovery counters are sidecar-bound.

## Common Pitfalls
- Using artifact hash as filename, synthesizing before reservation commit, finalizing before directory fsync, retrying unknown provider outcomes, ignoring orphan files or accepting zero ready personal cards.

## Stop-And-Challenge
- Stop on empty/zero-ready family, authority/profile/root/prestate drift, shared final path, mismatched staged/final bytes, unresolved journal/reservation, private leak or Phase 34 effect.

## Approval Gates
- Consume only Plan 12 powers and exact Phase 32 profile; optional human review/listening is not a readiness gate.

## Leverage Review
- Second pass traces every personal item and every audio reservation from process invocation through final path/evidence/recovery while proving no value leak or shared reference.
