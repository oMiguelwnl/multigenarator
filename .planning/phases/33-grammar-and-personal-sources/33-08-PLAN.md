# Phase 33 Plan 08: Grammar, Ordered-Source, and Review Repositories

<objective>
Implement PostgreSQL adapters that preserve immutable grammar roots, every ordered custom row, and atomic review revision history.

Purpose: Domain correctness must survive retries/concurrency and cannot depend on mutable in-memory collections.
Output: Three focused repositories and isolated PostgreSQL transaction/concurrency tests.
</objective>

## Anti-Goals
- No immutable-row updates, select-then-insert races, dropped positions, automatic bridge/content creation, or private-value copying.

## Hard Boundaries
- Parameterized SQLAlchemy only; one command appends evidence and advances exact versioned pointers atomically.

## Evidence Contract
- Round-trip, rehash, order, replay, conflict, rollback, two-session race, and unchanged-history tests must pass on PostgreSQL.

## Common Pitfalls
- `ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE` on authority, omitted ORDER BY input_position, or committing events before pointer CAS succeeds.

## Stop-And-Challenge
- Stop on schema/contract mismatch, non-atomic pointer behavior, history rewrite, hidden row loss, or retry/concurrency divergence.

## Approval Gates
- Writes remain isolated test/local operations; no production mutation is authorized.

## Leverage Review
- Lost: generic upserts. Kept: SQLAlchemy transactions and stable IDs. Gained: exact insert-only/replay-safe repositories.
