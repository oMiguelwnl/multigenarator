# Phase 33 Plan 06: Granular Item Outcomes and Truthful Stage Completion

<objective>
Model terminal state separately from attempt/processing facts and continue safely after item-local exceptions.

Purpose: GJOB-01 prevents coarse success flags from hiding review/media debt or losing all progress because one item failed.
Output: Extended job contracts, pure isolation/aggregation service, and exhaustive outcome/completion tests.
</objective>

## Anti-Goals
- No processed terminal enum/accepted alias, force-complete switch, sibling abort after item-local failure, unbounded retry, or raw exception persistence.

## Hard Boundaries
- Granular item truth drives aggregates; only item-local controlled failures continue, while systemic contract/config/authority errors stop.

## Evidence Contract
- Truth-table, mixed-run, stale-media, duplicate/defer, retry, systemic-stop, and secret-sentinel tests must all pass.

## Common Pitfalls
- Counting loop completion instead of persisted outcomes, hiding duplicates, or classifying every exception as recoverable.

## Stop-And-Challenge
- Stop on unclassifiable errors, inconsistent counts, outstanding obligations with requested completion, or unbounded resource policy.

## Approval Gates
- Review/media acceptance cannot be automated to satisfy the completion predicate.

## Leverage Review
- Lost: coarse success flags. Kept: stages/order/resume. Gained: auditable partial progress and exact completion truth.
