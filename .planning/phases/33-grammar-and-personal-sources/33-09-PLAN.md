# Phase 33 Plan 09: Immutable Private Excerpts and Persistent At-Most-Once Disclosure

<objective>
Replace Korean private-text mutation with immutable revisions and persist exact at-most-once context capabilities without copying private values.

Purpose: KPERS-02 and GREV-01 require replayable private history and atomic disclosure authority, not mutable rows or broad consent flags.
Output: Updated highlight repository, private-processing repository, broker adapter wiring, and privacy/concurrency tests.
</objective>

## Anti-Goals
- No private-text update, ordinary/eager exact-text read, broad capability, payload logging, live provider call, or unapproved purge policy.

## Hard Boundaries
- Changed Korean text appends a revision; safe validation precedes privileged load and one capability permits one bounded callback.

## Evidence Contract
- Revision/retry, safe-query, sentinel, two-session reservation race, transaction-boundary, unknown-result, idempotent-route, replay, and existing-mode tests must pass.

## Common Pitfalls
- Reusing mutable `upsert_import_records`, selecting ORM entities instead of safe columns, holding a transaction during callback, or resetting disclosing/failed_unknown for silent redisclosure.

## Stop-And-Challenge
- Stop on private leakage/eager load, stale race, inability to distinguish retry from change, or any required old-revision mutation.

## Approval Gates
- Production private imports and remote disclosure remain unauthorized; deterministic synthetic excerpts/fake callbacks only.

## Leverage Review
- Lost: mutable upsert and exactly-once assumptions. Kept: import/repository interfaces. Gained: immutable private history and at-most-once evidence.
