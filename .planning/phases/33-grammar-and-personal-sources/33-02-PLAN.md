# Phase 33 Plan 02: Ordered Custom-List Ledger and Explicit Prerequisite Decisions

<objective>
Preserve every Korean custom-list submission in order, resolve identity fail-closed, and expose bridge/defer/review decisions without silently changing the deck.

Purpose: KPERS-01 requires user intent and ambiguity to remain visible rather than being erased by normalized sets, key sorting, or heuristic identity.
Output: Ordered row contracts, compatible parser/fingerprint behavior, resolution/decision service, and focused negative tests.
</objective>

## Anti-Goals
- No row dropping, lexical collapse, automatic bridge generation, adaptive queue, provider identity fallback, or GUID/source-mode redesign.

## Hard Boundaries
- Position every nonblank row before duplicate/resolution logic; only exact local decisions may select bridge, defer, or needs_review.

## Evidence Contract
- Parser/fingerprint/identity/transition tests must cover repeats, reorder, inflection, compounds, ambiguity, policy drift, retry, and existing languages.

## Common Pitfalls
- Sorting before hashing, deduplicating after lemma resolution, losing submitted surface, or turning a proposal into a deck mutation.

## Stop-And-Challenge
- Stop on unavailable/inconclusive source identity, required GUID changes, or an unreviewed prerequisite-policy change.

## Approval Gates
- Bridge content requires explicit reviewed prerequisite references; this plan provides no generation or approval power.

## Leverage Review
- Lost: set/sort convenience. Kept: parser and Phase 30 identity. Gained: replayable ordered intent and explicit choices.
