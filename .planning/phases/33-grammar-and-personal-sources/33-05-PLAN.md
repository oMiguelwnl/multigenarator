# Phase 33 Plan 05: Immutable Field Revisions and Dependency-Scoped Review

<objective>
Define immutable field histories, exact review decisions, and local invalidation semantics shared by grammar and personal-source items.

Purpose: GREV-01 requires field-local regeneration without approval destruction, while KGRAM/KPERS content cannot become ready through coarse item flags.
Output: Review/revision contracts, pure transition service, dependency matrix, and concurrency/idempotency tests.
</objective>

## Anti-Goals
- No in-place content/review edits, auto-acceptance, history deletion, broad invalidation, or synthetic production review.

## Hard Boundaries
- Revisions/decisions/events append immutably; only exact versioned pointers move under CAS and explicit dependency hashes drive staleness.

## Evidence Contract
- Transition, mutation, concurrency, replay, dependency, and sentinel tests must prove retained history and unchanged unrelated fields.

## Common Pitfalls
- Storing approval on mutable content, invalidating all media after one field change, or advancing candidate and approved pointers together.

## Stop-And-Challenge
- Stop on ambiguous dependency, missing expected version, hash mismatch, or any historical update/delete request.

## Approval Gates
- Exact provider/model/route/prompt/schema/source hashes and 2/3-pass AI consensus are required for linguistic approval; human linguistic/listening UAT remains optional.

## Leverage Review
- Lost: overwrite convenience. Kept: hashes and deterministic IDs. Gained: exact append-only field history and scoped staleness.
